About

I am an undergraduate student at Tsinghua University working on structured reasoning in machine learning systems. My research focuses on knowledge graphs, large language models, and improving model reliability through better reasoning and evaluation.

Research Interests

  • Graph Learning & Knowledge Graphs
  • LLM Reasoning and Alignment
  • Relational Learning & Structured Data

Selected Projects

  • Knowledge Graph from Educational Texts
    Constructed a relational graph by extracting subject–predicate–object triples from structured educational content, and studied how to improve reasoning accuracy over noisy graph data.

  • LLM Reasoning with Reinforcement Learning
    Explored how supervision and reward signals affect reasoning consistency in language models.

  • Multimodal Model Reliability
    Analyzed model behavior under complex inputs and studied failure modes and hallucination in multimodal systems.
